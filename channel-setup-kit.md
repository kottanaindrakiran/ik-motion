## Setup checklist (do for each of the 4)

**YouTube:**
1. Create channel → set name, handle, upload ChatGPT logo (800x800) + banner (2560x1440)
2. Paste description → add channel keywords: (niche words, e.g. "geography, economics, explainer, facts")
3. Settings → Upload defaults: paste your standard hashtag block, category = Education
4. Link the Instagram account in channel "Links"
5. IMPORTANT: when uploading AI-narrated videos, tick the "Altered content / synthetic media" disclosure — keeps you policy-safe

**Instagram:**
1. Create account → switch to Professional (Creator) → category "Digital creator"
2. Same logo as profile picture, paste bio
3. Link YouTube channel in the bio link
4. First day: post 3 reels immediately (empty accounts don't get pushed)

**Naming consistency rule:** same handle on YT + IG for each niche, so fans find you everywhere.

## Test-month plan (all 4 channels, 30 days)

- Per channel daily: 2 Shorts (portrait, 30-60s) → post to BOTH YT Shorts + IG Reels
- Per channel weekly: 1 long video (3-5 min, landscape) on YouTube only
- Total: 8 renders/day + 4 long/week — about 2-3 hours daily including review
- Post times (IST): Shorts at 12pm & 7pm, long videos Saturday 6pm
- Track weekly in a sheet: views, avg watch %, subs per channel
- After 30 days: kill the bottom 2 channels, double down on the top 1-2

## What "clicking" looks like at day 30
- Winner: multiple 50k+ view Shorts, 1k+ subs, watch % over 70% on Shorts
- Keeper: steady 2-5k views, growing follows
- Kill: still under 500 views per video with no outliers
